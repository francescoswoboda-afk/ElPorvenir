El Porvenir Farm is dedicated to sustainable

Discover our farm ›

El Porvenir is a family owned and operated working pecan farm,
with over 20 years
experience in agriculture and farming and since 2014 we are chasing our passion for dry fruits.
The idea was born with the goal to oppose climate change and to grow healthy food for the human beings. Preserving the beauty of nature is another fundamental objective of our company and this is why our products are 100% Organic.
