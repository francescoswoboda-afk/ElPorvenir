02 · Our story

Before the first tree,
we studied the land.

  1. i

    Months of research

    Before planting anything, we spent months studying the regions where a pecan orchard could grow in the best possible conditions.

  2. ii

    Land that needed no clearing

    We chose land that did not require deforestation, in Monte Alverne near Santa Cruz do Sul, where the soil and climate suit pecans.

  3. iii

    Four varieties

    After years of study and field testing, we selected four varieties for their adaptability, productivity and resistance to disease and pests.

  4. iv

    Flowers for the bees

    Flowering plants grow throughout the fields to support bees and pollination, and to bring more life into the orchard.

  5. v

    Next: our own honey

    As the project grows, El Porvenir plans to produce its own raw honey, straight from the farm.
